## Data labeling

Working in the AI-Industry is usually associated with Silicon Valley. One thinks of coveted employees who are working for fancy start-ups in a pleasant environment. The wages are supposed to be high and the career perspective attractive. However, the AI-industry contains another workforce: this assignment is about the other, usually unseen, laborers of the AI-industry.

These laborers are not concerned with programming and developing new algorithms. These laborers are concerned with one of the driving forces of current AI: data. This data needs to be generated, annotated and enriched in order to improve AI-models. In addition, this practice of data labeling – outsourced by the AI-industry – is used to make AI-models such as ChatGPT ‘less toxic’, which implies labeling data that contains sexual or violent content. TIME Magazine described this in January 2023 in their article *‘Exclusive: OpenAI Used Kenyan Workers on Less Than $2 Per Hour to Make ChatGPT Less Toxic’* (Perrigo, 2023).

In this assignment you will analyze the practice of data labeling from the perspective of neocolonialism, a term already used in the 1960’s by Kwame Nkrumah, the author of *‘Neo-colonialism, the last stage of imperialism’* and president of Ghana from 1960 to 1966. In order to understand some of the different aspects of neocolonialism, please read the entry of the Encyclopedia Britannica regarding neocolonialism: [https://www.britannica.com/topic/neocolonialism](https://www.britannica.com/topic/neocolonialism). Next, read the TIME-article on Kenyan data-labeling workers: [https://time.com/6247678/openai-chatgpt-kenya-workers](https://time.com/6247678/openai-chatgpt-kenya-workers).

After reading these sources, please think about how the concept of neocolonialism can be applied to the current data labeling industry. Describe and argue to which degree the practices as described by *TIME* fit into the lens of neocolonialism. Be precise and illustrate your arguments with examples. In addition, you might mention, if applicable, aspects of neocolonialism that are not recurring in the data labeling industry.

Integrate your answers into a coherent text, containing around 500 words. The minimum requirement for the assignment is 350 words.
