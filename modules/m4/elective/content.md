
## Assistance hours

Using the form linked below, please indicate which days in the module you plan to
attend the assistance hours. You can select up to 2 days at most, and there is
no requirement of attendance on the selected days, but do please try to be accurate
in your estimate, as it helps us to schedule TA's and make sure there is space
for everyone. You're not required to select any days at all, but there might
not be space to ask questions on days you didn't select.
